Nothing bounces. Nothing spins. Motion communicates state changes and mass — a founder should never wait on an animation.

Presence model

The tree is state, not scenery

Full-screen on arrival. Then it demotes to a 56px sigil in the rail that keeps the same particle engine and reports what the system is doing: idle, thinking, speaking, awaiting you. A particle organism that never gets out of the way becomes a screensaver you resent by day four.

Voice model

Gesture unlocks the voice

Browsers block unprompted audio — a greeting that silently fails makes the AI feel broken on turn one. The "Wake Kalpavriksha" gesture is the permission grant, disguised as ceremony. Typing is the source of truth; speech rides on top and is always skippable.

Attention model

Ambient, never needy

After 18s the three choices fade up silently — no modal, no spoken question. Kalpavriksha only re-engages by voice if the founder has been idle and then moves the cursor: attention returned. An assistant that interrupts a CEO mid-thought is not a senior executive.

Concept · v1 · design milestone

This is a design artifact, not the product. Sample data throughout. Voice demo uses the browser speech engine; production would use a real TTS with a chosen executive voice.
